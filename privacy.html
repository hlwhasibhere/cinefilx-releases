Privacy policy

Google account and Drive access

When you connect Google Drive, Cinefilx uses your Google account profile and a read-only Drive authorization to list folders and video files, show file details, and stream videos you choose. Cinefilx does not edit or delete Drive files.

Data on your device

Cinefilx stores preferences such as the selected folder and may cache file details or playback information on your device. Video data is streamed from Google Drive to your device. The Android app does not send your Google profile, Drive contents, or access tokens to a Cinefilx-operated server. The app checks GitHub Releases for updates without sending your Google account or Drive authorization to GitHub.

Your choices

You can sign out in the app, revoke access from your Google Account connections, or clear Cinefilx’s app storage in Android Settings to remove app data stored on your device.

Google API data use

Cinefilx’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

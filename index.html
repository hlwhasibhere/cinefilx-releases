Cinefilx

Cinefilx is an Android video player for personal videos and other media you have permission to watch in your own Google Drive. The Google consent screen currently identifies the app as Personal Movie Player.

How Cinefilx uses Google Drive

Sign in with Google, choose a folder, and select a video to play. The app uses read-only Google Drive access to list folders and files, display video details, and stream the selected file directly from Drive to your Android device.

Cinefilx does not generate, modify, or distribute imagery. It has no AI image or video generation features, and it does not use Google APIs to create, edit, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery.
